Fixes and settings asked for since 1.0.1, on both the PAL and USA discs.

**Update:** open the launcher and press **Update**, or download **AgentUnderFireLauncher.exe** below if you are new. Your saves and settings are kept.

## New

- **Hit Flash setting** (PC Settings, Game page; also in F1): turn it off to stop the white flash when Bond is hit, on foot and in the
  driving missions. It is on by default, as in the original game. ([#7](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/7))
- **16:10 and 3:2 screens** (the Steam Deck, 1920x1200 monitors) are filled edge to edge instead of showing 16:9 with bars, the menus and
  HUD kept in proportion in the middle. ([#4](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/4))

## Fixed

- **R reloads mounted guns** in the on-rail missions (2 and 9). The controller's A reloads too, as before.
  ([#8](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/8))

## Also

- The launcher can update an install made the old way (built with the full toolchain) to the prebuilt one in place.
- Renderer fixes shared with the other ports.

Please report anything you find, with `AgentUnderFire\play\logs\auf.log` and any `.dmp` file beside the game.
