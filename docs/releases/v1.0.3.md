Cheats, a stairs fix and a faster renderer, on both the PAL and USA discs.

**Update:** open the launcher and press **Update**, or download **AgentUnderFireLauncher.exe** below if you are new. Your saves and settings are kept.

## New

- **Cheats** (PC Settings, new Cheats page): **Invincible** and **Unlimited Ammo**, for the missions on foot. Unlimited Ammo
  gives endless spare ammo for every weapon, the Golden Gun included; magazines still reload. More cheats are planned.
  ([#5](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/5))
- **More CPU cores used** for drawing, for smoother frame rates on busy scenes.

## Fixed

- **Stairs at high frame rates:** Bond climbs stairs at the same speed as at 60 FPS, and walks straight up them.
  ([#9](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/9))
- **Menu sounds with the mouse:** moving the pointer over the menus plays one move sound, not a burst of random ones.
  ([#3](https://github.com/hk21x/007-agentunderfire-recomp/issues/3))

## Also

- Disc reads that fail are retried before giving up.

Please report anything you find, with `AgentUnderFire\play\logs\auf.log` and any `.dmp` file beside the game.
